About MEDBOT

MEDBOT is a smart, robotic medicine dispenser that combines scheduled dosing, face-verified identity checks, and caretaker notifications to help patients take the right medicine at the right time — and keep caretakers informed when they don't.

Key Features

Smart Compartments

Independent medicine compartments with live stock tracking and refill alerts.

Face Recognition

Multi-angle patient verification before medicine is marked as collected.

Smart Scheduling

Multiple daily schedules per medicine with reminders and countdowns.

Caretaker Alerts

Telegram and email notifications for missed doses, low stock and more.

Battery Monitoring

Real-time LiFePO4 voltage tracking with automatic safety cutoffs.

Hardware Automation

ESP32-driven dispensing and patient search, with full simulation mode when offline.

How It Works

  1. Register your patient profile and caretaker contact details.
  2. Add medicines and assign them to compartments.
  3. Set schedules — MEDBOT reminds, verifies, and dispenses automatically.
  4. Caretakers are notified for missed doses, low stock, or low battery.

Fixed Position Mode

MEDBOT stays stationary with its motors disabled. Scheduling, reminders, dispensing, face verification and notifications all remain fully active — only movement is switched off.

Move Mode

MEDBOT actively searches for the scheduled patient using PIR motion sensing and ultrasonic obstacle avoidance, verifying identity by face once the patient is found.

Battery Monitoring

A LiFePO4 pack is tracked in real time — percentage, voltage, and charging status — with automatic safety cutoffs. Below 20%, MEDBOT stops all movement and switches to Fixed Position Mode until it's charged.

Smart Medicine Compartments

Every medicine lives in its own independent compartment with live stock tracking, automatic low-stock and empty alerts, and one-tap refilling — so the right dose always comes from the right compartment.

Face Recognition

Multi-angle facial verification (front, slight left, slight right, up, down) confirms the correct patient is present before medicine is marked as collected — with explicit consent required before any face data is stored.

Notification System

Reminders, dispensing confirmations, missed-dose alerts, and low-stock/battery warnings reach the patient and caretaker over Telegram — with safe simulation mode when no bot credentials are configured.

Technology Used

Flask, SQLite, OpenCV, face_recognition, APScheduler, Telegram Bot API, ESP32, and a responsive vanilla HTML/CSS/JS frontend.

Project Innovation

MEDBOT combines scheduled dosing, physical robotics, and biometric verification into one system — and unlike many hardware-dependent prototypes, every feature runs and can be demonstrated entirely in software through Hardware Simulation Mode, even with no physical robot connected.
